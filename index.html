Hide and seek on a real map. The zone keeps shrinking, and every few minutes the seeker gets a ping.

How a round works
  • One of you creates a room and places the zone on the map. Everyone else joins with the 4-letter code.
  • Hiders get a head start. The seeker's screen stays dark until it ends.
  • At random moments a ping drops every hider's position onto the seeker's map, with a siren.
  • The zone shrinks and drifts. Stay outside it for 20 seconds and the seeker sees you live.
  • Found someone? Get within a couple of metres and tap Found next to their name. The game checks both phones' GPS, so nobody can call a catch from across the park. Caught hiders join the hunt.
  • Safe points open at random, far from every hider. The first hider inside can't be caught for a few minutes, watches the seekers live and earns a jammer. The catch: the seekers watch them live too.
  • Each hider carries a jammer: arm it to vanish from the next ping.

Keep this page open with the screen on. Phones stop sharing location when the browser is in the background.
